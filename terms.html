Nalado helps you organize shopping lists, household inventory, tasks and events. Core features are free and work locally without a Nalado account. Optional Nalado Plus unlocks additional features. Current versions do not contain advertising.

You may use the app for lawful purposes. Only add photos or other material that you are entitled to use. These terms do not transfer ownership of your entries or photos to the developer.

2. Local records and backups

Your lists, inventory, photos and other records are stored on your device. Separate homes are local workspaces, not shared family accounts. Nalado does not provide live cloud synchronization or a developer-hosted copy of these records.

Automatic local copies in Nalado are enabled by default if you have not made a previous choice; an existing choice to turn them off is respected. This does not enable Google cloud backup in Android. You can change Nalado's setting in the app. Cloud delivery and automatic restoration depend on Android, your device and system backup settings. An automatic copy can only be restored if it is available and delivered by the system. Instant Google Drive downloads and guaranteed cloud restoration are not offered.

You can also save and restore a manual file copy. Manual copies are not encrypted by Nalado and may contain personal records and photos. Keep them securely. Before uninstalling, clearing app data or changing devices, we recommend saving a manual copy and checking that it is available.

Nalado Plus is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. There is no automatic renewal or recurring payment. Available features and the total price in your currency are shown before you confirm payment in Google Play.

Google Play processes payment; RevenueCat verifies access. Nalado does not receive your card details. Purchasing or restoring a purchase requires an internet connection and the Google Play account used for that purchase. A local data backup is not proof of purchase. Access may change following a refund or revocation of the purchase.

Support may also grant Plus without a purchase. Such access is associated with the current anonymous RevenueCat ID and the duration granted. This ID is not a login. After reinstalling or using another device, the ID may change and support may need to issue the gift again. A change in Plus access does not itself delete your existing local records.

If a purchase does not work, contact support or use the relevant Google Play support and refund procedures. Refund eligibility depends on applicable law, your location and the circumstances of the purchase; these terms do not impose a blanket no-refund rule. See Google Play refund information.

5. Catalogs, estimates and reminders

Catalog entries, barcode results, suggested expiry dates and replenishment estimates can be incomplete or inaccurate. Check product labels, storage conditions and the product itself. These features are planning aids, not a guarantee of food or medicine safety, medical advice or a replacement for professional advice.

Reminder delivery depends on notification permission, Android settings, battery restrictions and device operation. Do not rely on Nalado as the sole reminder for time-critical or safety-critical actions. New online barcode lookups depend on Open Food Facts and an internet connection; not every product will be found.

The separate Privacy Policy describes local storage, Android backup, photos, barcode lookup, purchase verification and support correspondence.
