Nalado — Privacy policy

Updated: 2026-10-21

About the app and contacting the developer

This policy applies to Nalado — Organizer, previously Organizer, developed by Nalado Studio. Contact the developer with privacy questions or requests to delete support correspondence at organizer.app.support@gmail.com.

Data on your device

Shopping lists, home inventory, storage areas, events, tasks, history and settings are processed locally and stored in the app area on your device. No account is required. Nalado has no server storing these records and does not automatically send them to the developer. Builds with monetization enabled use advertising and purchase verification as described below. There is no separate service analyzing your lists or automatically uploading their contents. It does not request access to contacts, location, microphone or camera.

Advertising and privacy choices

When monetization is enabled, the free version may show a Google AdMob banner. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may transmit an IP address (to estimate a region), device identifiers, ad interaction and diagnostic information to serve and measure ads and prevent abuse. Personalization and available choices depend on your region and preferences. Where required, the app displays a Google UMP form and an Ad privacy settings entry to change your choices. Non-personalized advertising may also involve data processing. Shopping, item, task and event names and notes are not sent as advertising attributes. Once an ad-free purchase is confirmed, the app stops requesting ads. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. When monetization is off, the app does not initiate advertising or purchase requests.

Ad-free purchase

Removing ads is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play, not a subscription. RevenueCat verifies and restores purchases. It receives a random SDK user identifier, app and device information, transaction details and purchase status. No Nalado account or email address is needed. The payment system handles card information; the app does not receive card details. Local lists and inventory are not sent to RevenueCat. Confirmed access is cached for offline use; restoring after reinstalling requires a connection and the Google Play account used for the purchase. Access may be revoked after a refund or purchase revocation. An exported inventory backup is not proof of payment. Provider policies: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/ and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Backups

You choose when to create a backup. It contains your entries and history as an unencrypted JSON file: anyone who obtains it can read its contents. You choose its destination or the app used to share it. If you select a cloud drive or another service, that service's data practices also apply. Android's built-in automatic cloud backup is disabled in the app configuration. Restoring a file replaces current records after confirmation. The language preference is not included in portable backups.

Feedback

Sending feedback opens the email or other app you choose. You can review and edit the message, and you confirm sending it yourself. Support receives the sender's address, message and information you attach. Optional technical details are off by default; they contain diagnostic information, not your item or event names or a database backup. Nalado does not read your mailbox or obtain its password. Email transmission and storage are also subject to the email providers' practices, including Gmail used by support.

Use and disclosure of information

Information you send is used to respond to your request, diagnose issues and fix errors. It is not sold or used for advertising profiling. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or medical information. Share a backup with support only by separate agreement if the problem cannot be reproduced without it. Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law.

Notifications and widgets

Event reminders are scheduled locally. You may decline notification permission or revoke it in Android settings. The app may restore reminder schedules after a device restart. Event names and selected list contents can appear in notifications and widgets; lock-screen settings and access to your phone affect their privacy.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them. Archiving is not deletion. Clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling removes the local database; back up records you want to keep first. This does not delete exported files or previously sent emails: manage those separately. Support correspondence is retained for as long as necessary to handle the request, related fixes and legal obligations. To request its deletion, contact support, preferably from the address you originally used. The developer cannot remotely read or delete your local database. Uninstalling does not delete Google Play purchase history or RevenueCat purchase verification records. Their retention depends on provider policies and accounting and fraud-prevention requirements. Contact support about deleting purchase verification data.

Security and policy updates

Android mechanisms restrict access to local app data. Nalado does not promise separate encryption of the database or exported backups. Protect your phone with a screen lock and keep backups in a safe place. This policy will be updated if data practices change; its revision date is shown at the beginning. This version has no cloud synchronization or subscriptions. Adding advertising networks or other services requires a policy update.
